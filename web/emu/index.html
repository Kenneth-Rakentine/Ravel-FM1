Ravel FM-1 emulator

Playing

  • Press Sound on, then play the keys with a mouse, touch, or your computer keyboard: A is C4, the row A–; plays white keys and W E T Y U O P the black keys.
  • [ and ] shift the octave. PLAY holds notes.
  • Drag a knob up or down, or scroll over it.

Pages

  • HOME tracks, EDIT voice, ARP tape, GLO mixer. SELECT steps through a page's sections, ALGO picks the track.
  • K1–K4 change the four parameters shown at the top of the screen.
  • REC catches the tape: the grains keep playing what it holds. SAVE keeps the sound in this browser.

What this is

The Ravel firmware's own code, compiled to WebAssembly: the same synth voice, Beads grain engine and screen drawing that run on the FM-1, on a simulated panel. Only the hardware drivers are replaced.
